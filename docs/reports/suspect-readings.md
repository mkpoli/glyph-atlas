# How often a mark's reading is right

A suspect mark (`atlas review suspects`) says that the classifier gives a crop's label less than 5% and, for most, names
the character it reads instead (`reads_as`). Quick Review shows that character on the tile. This page measures how
often a reviewer took it.

## Data

The site's review export (`/atlas/reviews.json`) held 4,024 reviews on 2026-11-25. 14 have no record. 557 are on crops
marked on 2026-09-26; 91 of those were superseded by a later review, 106 were given on another label than the mark's and
8 on another box. Of the 352 left, 281 are on a mark that names a character. Crops passed unmarked in a round are not
reviews and are not counted.

## Second signals

198 of the 281 are in the similar-crop index of 2026-09-27. Neighbours are the 20 nearest crops of other pages, under the
characters they are filed under on 2026-11-25; three marks whose reading and label are variants of one another are left out.

| Nearest crops in a row that carry the reading | Crops | Grapheme right |

Of the 13 misses where all 20 carry it, 8 are a bad crop or a box redrawn around its own character.

Where one of the two crops before or after it in its line is filed under the reading, the reading's grapheme is right for
26 of 30 crops, against 65 of 222 where none is. This is a label shifted along its line. The neighbours' labels are today's, some of
them corrected in the same pass, so the figure is an upper one.

## Reading

A mark finds crops that need a person: 22 of 281 were simply right. It does not say what is wrong with them. No threshold measured here
makes a bulk "take every reading" safe; a reading is an offer on one tile. Reviewers chose the crops they opened, so the
figures describe reviewed marks, and the share differs by month with the faults being worked on.

## Readings that were no doubt at all

Of 27,599 pending marks that name a character, 1,140 name the kanji a kana label derives from (𛂙 read as 乃, 296; 𛂚 as 濃, 103;
や as 也, 66). On 18 sampled crops, looked at enlarged, every label was right. The rule now expects that reading, as it
already did for a kanji read as its kana.

414 name the 字母 of another kana of the label's sound: と read as 登 (262), ひ as 日 (20), く as 具 (9). All 12 sampled と crops
are written in the form derived from 登. The mark now names the kana where the 字母 gives one (く → 𛀰) and none where it gives several.

218 name another form of the label's grapheme (淸 read as 清, 羣 as 群). Most of the 12 sampled show the form named, so these marks stay.
