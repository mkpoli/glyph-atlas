# How often a mark's reading is right

A suspect mark (`atlas review suspects`) says that the classifier gives a crop's label less than 5% and, for most, names
the character it reads instead (`reads_as`). Quick Review shows that character on the tile. This page measures how
often the character a reviewer named is the one the mark names.

## Data

The site's review export (`/atlas/reviews.json`) held 4,024 reviews on 2026-11-25. 14 have no record. 557 are on crops
marked on 2026-09-26; 91 of those were superseded by a later review, 106 were given on another label than the mark's and
8 on another box. Of the 352 left, 281 are on a mark that names a character, 72 of them given before the marks were made.
Crops passed unmarked in a round are not reviews and are not counted.

## Second signals

196 of the 281 are in the similar-crop index of 2026-09-27 and have a reading that is no variant of the label. The label
of 70 has changed since, many in the same pass as their neighbours, so a signal read from today's labels is partly the
correction itself. Both are given; the labels of 27 September are the fair ones.

| Signal | Labels of 27 September | Labels of 25 November |
|---|---|---|
| 1 or more nearest crops in a row carry the reading | 41 of 92 | 53 of 105 |
| 5 or more | 21 of 40 | 45 of 70 |
| 12 or more | 12 of 23 | 33 of 48 |
| All 20 | 9 of 19 | 28 of 41 |
| One of the two crops before or after it in its line is filed under the reading | 46 of 56 | 21 of 25 |
| None of them is | 13 of 139 | 38 of 170 |
| The line neighbour, and probability 0.0000 | 45 of 53 | 20 of 22 |

Neighbours are the 20 nearest crops of other pages. Nearest crops do not separate the right readings.

A line neighbour with the reading does: 45 of 53 where the probability is 0.0000 (85%, 95% interval 73–92%). The 43 of
those reviewed after the marks come from seven `ar:` books, so the figure may not hold elsewhere.

489 pending marks are of this kind on 2026-11-25. 460 are of collected crops (343 `ar:`, 117 `hk:`; two were reviewed on the site before
this was done) in 425 lines, 391 of them with a single one; the other 29 are corpus glyphs and were left. They are no
lines slipped as a whole: beside most of them another box of the line is cut wrong. Each was looked at enlarged in its context.
240 were judged to be exactly the character named, whole in its box and in that form; one was reviewed meanwhile and six べ and ぺ could be
either script, which leaves 233. A second reader was shown those 233 with the proposed character and without the label, knew that a first
reader had passed them, and rejected 24 and was unsure of 33. The 176 both accepted are the relabels, less 2 whose new character is a
symbol. The rest are mostly a box over part of a stroke (し), a form of another parent (の from 能, す from 須) or a variant
(嶋 read as 島, 皈 as 帰).

## Reading

A mark's reading does not say what is wrong with the crop. No threshold measured here makes a bulk "take every reading" safe; a
reading is an offer on one tile.

## Readings that were no doubt at all

Of 27,599 pending marks that name a character, 1,159 name the kanji a hiragana or hentaigana label derives from
(𛂙 read as 乃, 296; 𛂚 as 濃, 103; や as 也, 66). On 18 sampled crops, looked at enlarged, every label was right. The rule
now expects that reading, as it already did for a kanji read as its kana.

404 name the 字母 of another kana of the label's sound: と read as 登 (262), く as 具, ら as 羅. All 12
sampled と crops are written in the form derived from 登; く, ひ and し crops were mixed. The mark stays and names no
character.

218 name another form of the label's grapheme (淸 read as 清, 羣 as 群). Most of the 12 sampled show the form named, so these
marks stay. A katakana read as its own parent (ヰ as 井, 4 of 4 right) stays marked: katakana take part of a kanji, and only
ヰ and a few more are whole.
