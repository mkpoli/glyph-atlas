Reading of the evidence

    From crop to shape

    Each crop is restored to clean ink and compared by a model trained to ignore ink weight, dry-brush texture, broken strokes, slant and position. Whether a crop is a written __CHAR__ at all is left to the site's character classifier.

    1. Pick the crisp crops: high contrast, sharp edges, few specks, at most four pieces of ink. Their ink, with gaps closed and edges smoothed, is the clean state.
    2. Train a restoration network on copies of those crops made to look worse: faint, blurred or dry-brush ink, stained or grainy paper, specks, blots and fragments of a neighbouring character. It learns to return the clean ink.
    3. Restore every crop, drop specks and edge fragments, trim it to the ink and fit it into a 96-pixel frame, keeping its proportions.
    4. Train an encoder on the restored images alone: two randomly altered copies of a crop must be matched to each other and to no other crop. The alterations change position, size, proportions, slant, ink weight, broken strokes, blur, specks and stray fragments, so the encoder learns to disregard them.
    5. Decide written __CHAR__ or abbreviation with the site's own character classifier, which reads the character better than any shape measure; sort the abbreviations by shape (steps 1–4).

    Crop by crop: site clusters against forms

    Site clusters (C1–C__NSITE__)

    The site's character classifier is trained to tell characters apart. Each crop of __CHAR__ passes through it, and the layer just before its answer is kept as a 512-number description of the crop. Spherical k-means then splits the crops into __NSITE__ groups by those descriptions (method classifier-penultimate/spherical-kmeans-v1, revision __REVISION__). Because the classifier learnt to give every way of writing __CHAR__ the same answer, it recognises a written __CHAR__ reliably, but the differences left between groups are partly form and partly paper, crop box and ink weight.

    Proposed forms

    The same classifier decides only whether a crop is a written __CHAR__ or an abbreviation. Each abbreviation is restored to clean ink and described by an encoder trained on the __CHAR__ crops to disregard paper, ink weight, texture and position, so only the stroke shape is left to compare. A Ward tree groups those descriptions into shape clusters, and its main branches become the forms.

    Method and sources

    Crops
    Glyph Atlas forms page for __CHAR__, glyphatlas.org/en/forms/__CODE__, clustering revision __REVISION__; C numbers are the site's cluster numbers. Pixels cut from the corpus scans behind that page, at most 160 px on the long side.
    Restoration
    U-Net (Ronneberger, Fischer & Brox 2015, arXiv:1505.04597), four levels, 32–256 channels, trained from scratch for __STEPS__ steps on __CRISP__ crisp crops of __CHAR__ (contrast ≥ 0.55, edge sharpness ≥ 0.35, specks < 2% of ink, ≤ 4 pieces, ink already near its smoothed form). Targets: Otsu ink without specks and edge fragments, closed and smoothed (Gaussian σ 1.2). Inputs: the same crops, synthetically degraded. Binary cross-entropy plus Dice loss, 96 px. Thresholding and filtering with scikit-image 0.26.
    Encoder
    ResNet-18 (He et al. 2016, arXiv:1512.03385; timm resnet18, one input channel, no pretrained weights) trained contrastively on the __USABLE__ restored crops of __CHAR__ alone, with no labels: SimCLR objective (Chen et al. 2020, arXiv:2002.05709), temperature 0.1, batch 512, __EPOCHS__ epochs at 64 px. Two encoders were trained, the second with stronger ink-weight changes, and their 512-d features joined, centred and L2-normalised. Replacing hand-written cleaning with a learned model follows a suggestion by 金沢クレハ (刈羽マキ) on Discord, 24 October 2026 (personal communication, unpublished), who proposed a variational autoencoder followed by k-means; a contrastive encoder was used instead so that pixel layout does not dominate the features.
    __CHAR__ or abbreviation
    Tree
    UMAP to 10 dimensions (McInnes, Healy & Melville 2018, arXiv:1802.03426; n_neighbors 30, min_dist 0, cosine, seed 0), then SciPy Ward linkage over all crops. The 24 leaves are the tree cut into 24 groups; the branches above them are the tree's own top merges. Branch lengths use a square-root scale.
    3-D map
    UMAP of the 10-d coordinates to 3-d (n_neighbors 30, min_dist 0.2). Local neighbourhoods are kept; distances between far groups are not to scale.
